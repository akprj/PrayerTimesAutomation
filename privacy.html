Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Prayer Times Automation is operated by Nouman Ahmad to help Jamaat Ahmadiyya members add prayer timetable events to their Google Calendar.

Data we access and use

With your authorization, the app accesses calendar information needed to select a destination calendar, check existing prayer events, and create or update prayer events. It may process your Google account email, calendar IDs, and event dates and times. The app does not receive your Google password.

Authorization and timetable processing

The app stores OAuth credentials, including refresh tokens where provided, to perform scheduled updates without asking you to sign in each time. It reads timetable images from a designated Google Drive folder using a service account. Images are processed in memory rather than permanently saved by the timetable-fetching feature. This does not require access to members' personal Drive files.

Sharing and protection

Data is used to provide and maintain prayer-calendar automation, not for advertising or sale. Google and the hosting or storage providers used to operate the app process data as necessary to deliver the service. Credentials must be kept in access-controlled storage and must not be published in website files or source-code repositories.

Retention and deletion

Account configuration and authorization credentials are retained while needed to provide scheduled updates. To stop the service and request deletion of app-held account data, email noumanahmad1987@gmail.com . Events already added to Google Calendar remain unless you delete them separately.

Your control

You can revoke access at any time through Google Account connections . Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete data already held by the app.

Google API data

Prayer Times Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact noumanahmad1987@gmail.com . Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
