Connect Your Google Calendar For Prayer Times Reminder

Prayer Times Automation

Helping Jamaat Ahmadiyya members keep prayer timetable events in their Google Calendar.

About the app

Prayer Times Automation reads prayer timetable images from a designated Google Drive folder and uses the extracted dates and times to create or update prayer events in your selected Google Calendar, with your permission.

It is intended to reduce manual entry and help members keep track of their local prayer schedule.

How it works

  1. Authorize Calendar access through Google's sign-in and consent process in the application.
  2. The app reads the prayer timetable and identifies its dates and prayer times.
  3. Prayer events are added to your selected calendar. Stored authorization allows scheduled updates without signing in each time.

This website provides information about the app. It does not itself connect your Google account. Contact the developer below for access.

Google Calendar access

The app requests Calendar permissions to find the destination calendar, check existing prayer events, and create or update prayer events. Your Google password is not provided to the app.

You can revoke its access at any time through your Google Account connections . Read our Privacy Policy for details about data use.

Please check your local timetable

Image recognition and automated updates can produce errors or be delayed. Please compare calendar events with your Jamaat's published timetable. The published timetable should be your reference if there is a discrepancy.
