🔔 How to Add Prayer Notifications Manually
** Only Applicable to QR/NFC Scan & Add directly Method **

Because modern smartphone privacy rules prevent shared public subscription feeds from creating loud alarm sounds automatically, Method 1 users must perform this quick one-time setup to enable sound reminders before prayer times:

  1. Open your phone's Google Calendar app (or go to calendar.google.com in a web browser).
  2. Tap the Menu icon (three horizontal lines ☰) in the top-left corner.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the menu and choose Settings (⚙️).
  4. Under the settings list, select Bait-us-Subuh Timings.
  5. Tap on Add a notification under the default notifications section.
  6. Select your preferred reminder time (e.g., 15 minutes before, or 30 minutes before) to ensure your device plays an audio alert for upcoming prayers.

About the app

Prayer Times Automation supports two distinct mechanisms for adding prayer schedules to your calendar:

  • Direct Add Method (QR Code / NFC Tag): Utilizes read-only public iCal/Webcal calendar feed links. Scanning or tapping directly imports and synchronizes pre-generated prayer events into your mobile calendar application without requiring account authentication or stored access tokens.
  • Google Account Integration Method: Uses secure OAuth 2.0 authentication to request permission to manage events in your primary Google Calendar. The background service reads local prayer timetable images from a managed Drive directory, uses OCR/computer vision processing to extract dates and times, and directly constructs or updates dedicated prayer event entries in your authenticated personal Google Calendar.

Both methods are designed to reduce manual data entry and ensure members keep track of their local prayer timetable accurately.

Method 1: Direct Add (QR Code/NFC Tag)

Your physical QR code and NFC tag are programmed to add all prayer times and reminder events straight to your smartphone calendar instantly—bypassing any manual setup.

How the direct process works:

  1. Direct URL/iCal Protocol Encoding: The physical NFC tag and printed QR code are encoded with a direct public calendar feed URI scheme (using Webcal / iCal subscription links for iOS and direct Google Calendar rendering prompts for Android).
  2. Instant Public Calendar Subscription: Scanning or tapping instantly prompts your native calendar app to subscribe to a read-only public master feed. All schedule updates published to the master feed reflect automatically in your personal calendar without needing manual adjustments.

Method 2: Google Account Integration

  1. Connect Calendar via Google Account Login. Use the button above to authorize OAuth 2.0 access, allowing the app to safely manage your designated prayer calendar entries.

How to disconnect Prayer Times App's Google Calender

Choose the instructions for the way you added the prayer calendar. QR Code/NFC adds a calendar subscription to your calendar app, while Google Account login gives the app permission to manage events in your account.

Method-1 Users:

Remove the Bait-us-Subuh Timings calender added via QR Code / NFC by unsubscribing it

If you added the calendar via QR Code/NFC, unsubscribe from it to stop seeing its events:

  1. Open Google Calendar in a web browser and sign in to the account where you added the subscription.
  2. In the left-hand panel, find the prayer timetable under Other calendars. Its displayed name may be “Bait-us-Subuh Timings”.
  3. Hover over that calendar, select the three-dot menu, then choose Settings.
  4. In the calendar settings, scroll to Remove calendar, choose Unsubscribe, and confirm if asked.

If you added the calendar in Apple Calendar or another calendar app, remove the subscription in that app's Calendars or Subscriptions settings; menu names vary by device.

Method-2 Users:

Remove Google Account login access

  1. Open Google Account connections and sign in to the same Google account you connected to the app.
  2. Find Prayer Times Automation (or the app name shown in your connections), open its details, and choose Remove access or Delete all connections.
  3. Confirm the removal. Google will revoke the app's permission to access that account's Calendar.

Revoking access stops the app from making future changes to your account after Google's revocation takes effect.
