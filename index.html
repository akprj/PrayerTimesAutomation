About the app

Prayer Times Automation reads prayer timetable images from a designated Google Drive folder and uses the extracted dates and times to create or update prayer events in your selected Google Calendar, with your permission.

It is intended to reduce manual entry and help members keep track of their local prayer schedule.

How it works

  1. Connect your Google Calendar. Use the button above to start Google's sign-in and consent process and review the requested permissions.
  2. Read the prayer timetable. The application reads the designated timetable image and identifies its dates and prayer times.
  3. Create or update prayer events. The extracted schedule is used to add or update events in your selected Google Calendar.
  4. Keep your schedule up to date. Stored authorization allows configured scheduled updates without asking you to sign in each time.

Please review the permissions shown by Google before granting access. You can revoke the application's access at any time through your Google Account settings.

Google Calendar access

The app requests Calendar permissions to find the destination calendar, check existing prayer events, and create or update prayer events.

Your Google password is not provided to the app. Authorization is handled through Google's sign-in and consent process.

You can revoke access through your Google Account connections . Read our Privacy Policy for details about data use.

Please check your local timetable

Image recognition and automated updates can produce errors or be delayed. Please compare calendar events with your Jamaat's published timetable.

The published timetable should be your reference if there is a discrepancy. Calendar reminders also depend on your Google Calendar notification settings and device permissions.
