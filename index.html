Direct Master Calendar QR Code & NFC Tag

Your physical QR code and NFC tag are programmed to add all prayer times and reminder events straight to your smartphone calendar instantly—bypassing any manual setup.

How the direct process works:

  1. Direct URL/iCal Protocol Encoding: The physical NFC tag and printed QR code are encoded with a direct public calendar feed URI scheme (using Webcal / iCal subscription links for iOS and direct Google Calendar rendering prompts for Android).
  2. Instant Public Calendar Subscription: Scanning or tapping instantly prompts your native calendar app to subscribe to a read-only public master feed. All schedule updates published to the master feed reflect automatically in your personal calendar.

About the app

Prayer Times Automation supports two distinct mechanisms for adding prayer schedules to your calendar:

  • Direct Add Method (QR Code / NFC Tag): Utilizes read-only public iCal/Webcal calendar feed links. Scanning or tapping directly imports and synchronizes pre-generated prayer events into your mobile calendar application without requiring account authentication or stored access tokens.
  • Google Account Integration Method: Uses secure OAuth 2.0 authentication to request permission to manage events in your primary Google Calendar. The background service reads local prayer timetable images from a managed Drive directory, uses OCR/computer vision processing to extract dates and times, and directly constructs or updates dedicated prayer event entries in your authenticated personal Google Calendar.

Both methods are designed to reduce manual data entry and ensure members keep track of their local prayer timetable accurately.

How it works

  1. Connect Calendar via Google Account Login. Use the button above to authorize OAuth 2.0 access, allowing the app to safely manage your designated prayer calendar entries.
  2. Read the prayer timetable. The background system parses the designated published timetable image and extracts prayer dates and timings using automated image processing.
  3. Create or update prayer events. Extracted schedules are synchronized directly to your personal Google Calendar or broadcasted through the public master feed.
  4. Keep your schedule up to date. Automated background routines run periodically to update upcoming prayer events without requiring repeated sign-ins.

Please review the permissions shown by Google before granting access. You can revoke the application's access at any time through your Google Account settings.

Google Calendar access

When using the Google Account login method, the app requests Calendar permissions solely to identify destination calendars, check existing entries, and create or update prayer schedule reminders.

Your Google account password is never collected or stored. Authorization is securely handled through Google's official OAuth 2.0 consent interface.

You can revoke access anytime through your Google Account connections .

Please check your local timetable

Automated image recognition and updates can occasionally experience delays or discrepancies. Please verify your calendar reminders against your local Jamaat's official published timetable.

The official published timetable remains your primary reference if any discrepancy occurs. Calendar notifications also depend on your smartphone's notification and power management settings.
