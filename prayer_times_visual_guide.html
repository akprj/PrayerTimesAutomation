Bait-us-Subuh · Simple setup guide

Prayer Times Automation

Choose how to add the timetable, then follow the matching reminder and sound steps.

Choose one of two ways to connect

Both methods put Bait-us-Subuh prayer events in your calendar. Method 1 does not require Google sign-in. Method 2 uses your Google account to create and update events in your calendar.

Method 1 · no sign-in

Add the public calendar subscription

Use either option below. They both add the same Bait-us-Subuh calendar.

Option 1: Scan the QR code shown on the app website.
  1. Scan the QR code shown on a poster or another screen.
  2. Tap the link or calendar prompt that appears.
  3. Choose Add or Subscribe to confirm.
Option 2: Tap “Click to Add without SCAN directly”.
  1. Open the app website on your phone.
  2. Tap the green direct-add button under the QR code.
  3. Confirm by choosing Add or Subscribe.
Method 2 · Google sign-in

Connect your Google Calendar

  1. Tap Add Prayer Times via Google Login (OAuth).
  2. Choose the Google account where you want the events.
  3. Review and approve the requested Calendar access.
  4. Return to the app and wait for the connection confirmation.

Good to know: You sign in on Google’s page. Your Google password is not given to the Prayer Times Automation app.

This method reads the current Bait-us-Subuh timetable image from the configured Google Drive folder, processes the prayer times, and syncs events to your connected calendar.

Next: Method 1 needs a one-time default reminder rule on your phone. Method 2 already adds reminders to events. Both methods use your device’s calendar notification settings for sound.

Method 1 · one-time reminder setup

Set your prayer alert time

Choose how long before each event you want a notification. This is a calendar default rule, not a phone alarm.

Do this after adding the calendar. In Google Calendar, set a default notification for the Bait-us-Subuh calendar. It applies to events on that calendar for your account. Choose your preferred lead time, such as 15 minutes before.

Android · Google Calendar app

Set a default notification

Google Calendar → ☰ Menu → Settings → Bait-us-Subuh Timings
  1. Open the Google Calendar app.
  2. Tap ☰ Menu, then Settings.
  3. Under your calendars, tap Bait-us-Subuh Timings.
  4. Tap Add a notification under event notifications.
  5. Choose your preferred time, for example 15 minutes before.
  6. Make sure Android allows Google Calendar notifications.
iPhone / iPad · Google Calendar app

Set a default notification

Google Calendar → ☰ Menu → Settings → Bait-us-Subuh
  1. Open the Google Calendar app and sign in to the Google account where you added the calendar.
  2. Tap ☰ Menu, then Settings.
  3. Select Bait-us-Subuh Timings from the calendar list.
  4. Tap Add another notification.
  5. Choose your preferred lead time, such as 15 minutes before.
  6. On iPhone, open Settings → Notifications → Google Calendar and allow notifications and sounds.

One-time setup, with a useful distinction

The notification is a default for events on this calendar in your Google account; it is not a permanent guarantee for every event in every app. Confirm that notifications are enabled and that future prayer events show the alert you selected.

If the calendar is missing from Settings, check that it is visible in the Google Calendar app and that the correct Google account is selected.

Method 2 · Google account connection

Automatic timetable and reminders

After you approve access, the service keeps your connected Google Calendar in sync with the current timetable.

What happens after you connect?

1 · Timetable imageThe current Bait-us-Subuh timetable picture is placed in the configured Google Drive folder.
2 · Time readingThe service reads the timetable image and identifies its dates and prayer times.
3 · Calendar syncPrayer events are created or updated in the Google Calendar you connected.
4 · Reminder addedEach event receives its configured reminder automatically.
Daily prayers
15 minutes before

Default reminder for Fajr, Zohar/Zuhr, Assr/Asr, Maghrib, and Ishaa/Isha.

Friday prayer
90 minutes before

Default reminder for the Friday Juma/Jummah event.

No manual reminder rule is needed for Method 2. These lead times are included by the service when it creates or updates each prayer event. To hear a sound, allow Google Calendar notifications on your phone and follow Page 4.

Quick check after connecting

The correct Google account was selected.
The Prayer Times calendar is visible.
A daily event shows a 15-minute reminder.
A Friday Juma event shows a 90-minute reminder.

Sound settings · both methods

Choose your notification sound

The reminder time and the sound are separate settings. Sound is controlled by your phone and calendar app.

The app does not contain an Azan player or sound file. It creates calendar events and reminders; your phone’s calendar notification system plays the alert.

Android · choose a tone or audio file

Google Calendar notification sound

Phone Settings → Apps → Google Calendar → Notifications → Calendar notifications → Sound
  1. Open the phone’s Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps (menu names vary by manufacturer), then Google Calendar.
  3. Tap Notifications, then Calendar notifications or the event reminder category.
  4. Choose Sound and select a tone. If your phone offers a file browser, choose a downloaded Azan or other audio file.
  5. If the file is not listed, follow your phone maker’s instructions for adding notification sounds; some Android phones require it in the Notifications or Ringtones folder.
iPhone / iPad · Google Calendar

Allow the calendar alert sound

iPhone Settings → Notifications → Google Calendar → Allow Notifications / Sounds
  1. Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Google Calendar.
  2. Turn on Allow Notifications and Sounds. Choose an alert style if offered.
  3. In Google Calendar, open ☰ Menu → Settings → Bait-us-Subuh to check its event notifications.

Custom Azan limitation: Google Calendar on iPhone does not provide Android’s “browse the phone for an MP3” sound picker. Enable app sounds in Notifications. If your iOS version offers Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Default Alerts, you can choose a built-in system alert tone; it may not override sounds controlled by an individual app. An arbitrary downloaded MP3 cannot be assigned directly unless iOS and the app expose it as a selectable alert.

Before relying on an alert

Calendar notifications are allowed.
The phone is not muted or silencing alerts with Focus / Do Not Disturb.
The correct calendar is visible and syncing.
A test or upcoming event shows its reminder.

Google’s iPhone/iPad reminder steps: Change Google Calendar notifications. Android menu names and sound pickers vary by device and Android version.
