📱 What is this Application & What Does It Do?

Prayer Times Automation connects the official Bait-us-Subuh (Frankfurt am Main) prayer timetable directly to your personal smartphone calendar. When an updated schedule picture is uploaded to the mosque Google Drive, the automated OCR backend reads all times and synchronizes all 5 daily prayers (Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and Friday Jummah directly into your calendar.

✓ 100% Official Mosque Timings
✓ Weekly Auto Background Sync
✓ Supports Android & Apple iOS
⚠️

Clarification: The App Has No Built-In Azan Sound Player

The application itself does not contain built-in Azan audio files or an audio engine. All alarm sounds and rings are controlled entirely by your smartphone's Calendar app notification settings. To play an Azan MP3 or custom chime, follow Page 4 of this guide.

Why Do Method 1 Users Need This One-Time Setup?

Modern smartphone operating systems (Android & Apple iOS) deliberately prevent public calendar subscriptions from turning on loud alarms automatically without user consent. By configuring your notification time once in your calendar app settings, you establish a permanent lifetime rule for all 5 daily prayers (Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha)!

🤖

FOR ANDROID PHONES

Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Motorola
Google Calendar > Menu (☰) > Settings > Bait-us-Subuh
☰ Google Calendar ⚙️
CALENDAR SETTINGS
Bait-us-Subuh Timings
Default notifications:
🔔 15 minutes before ✓ Active
+ Add a notification
  1. Open the Google Calendar app on your phone.
  2. Tap the Menu icon (☰) in top-left corner.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings (⚙️).
  4. Select "Bait-us-Subuh Timings" calendar.
  5. Under default notifications, tap "Add a notification".
  6. Select your desired reminder time: e.g. 15 minutes before.
✓ Done! Google Calendar will now alert you for every prayer!
🍏

FOR APPLE IPHONE & IPAD

iPhone (all iOS versions) & iPad
Apple Calendar > Calendars > Info (i) > Event Alerts: ON
< Back Bait-us-Subuh Info
Calendar Name Bait-us-Subuh
Event Alerts ON
Default Alert Time 15 min before
  1. Open the built-in Calendar app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Calendars at the bottom center.
  3. Find and tap the Info icon (i) next to Bait-us-Subuh.
  4. Ensure "Event Alerts" toggle is switched ON.
  5. In iPhone Settings > Calendar > Default Alert Times, set Events to 15 minutes before (or use iOS Google Calendar app).
✓ Done! Your iPhone will now alert you for every prayer!

Important: How Sound Actually Works on Both Methods

Whether you connected via Method 1 (QR scan / direct button) or Method 2 (Google login), all notification ringtones are sounded exclusively by your phone's Calendar notification system. To hear an Azan or your favorite chime, simply select any audio file already stored on your device using the steps below!

🤖

ANDROID: GOOGLE CALENDAR SOUND

Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Motorola
Settings > Apps > Google Calendar > Notifications > Sound
App Notifications ⚙️
CALENDAR NOTIFICATIONS
Calendar notifications Active
Sound / Ringtone: 🎵 Azan_Makkah.mp3
+ Add custom audio / Azan MP3
  1. Open phone Settings (⚙️) > tap Apps.
  2. Tap Google Calendar > tap Notifications.
  3. Tap Calendar notifications > tap Sound.
  4. Tap "+" / "Add ringtone" to browse files.
  5. Select your downloaded Azan MP3 file!
💡 Tip: Copy your Azan MP3 into your phone's Notifications or Ringtones folder so it shows in the list.
🍏

APPLE iOS: CALENDAR ALERT SOUND

iPhone & iPad (Apple or Google Calendar app)
Settings > Notifications > Calendar > Sounds
< Back Calendar Notifications
Allow Notifications ON
Sounds / Alert Tone: 🔔 Azan Tone / Chime
Banners & Lock Screen ✓ Enabled
  1. Open iPhone Settings (⚙️) > tap Notifications.
  2. Scroll down and tap Calendar (or Google Calendar).
  3. Verify "Allow Notifications" is switched ON.
  4. Tap Sounds (or go to Sounds & Haptics).
  5. Select your desired chime or custom Azan tone!
💡 Tip: Ensure your iPhone physical mute switch is off and Calendar notifications are allowed in Settings > Focus.
