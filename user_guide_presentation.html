Overview · Purpose

What is this application?

A dedicated digital service that connects official mosque prayer schedules (such as Bait-us-Subuh) directly to members' personal phone calendars.

The Old Everyday Hassle

  • Prayer times change weekly as the sun shifts and seasonal daylight saving starts or ends.
  • Manually setting five alarms every few days on your phone clock is time-consuming and easy to forget.
  • Paper timetables or static PDF photos get lost or forgotten in chat messages.

The Automated Solution

  • Automated Reading: The system automatically processes the official weekly timetable.
  • Direct Calendar Sync: Fajr, Zuhr/Jummah, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha are placed neatly into your calendar.
  • Combined timetable times: When the timetable fallback identifies a shared Zohar/Assr or Maghrib/Isha time, it appears as one combined calendar entry rather than two separate entries.
  • Zero Maintenance: Once connected, you never have to re-enter dates or times again.

Result: Your phone calendar always shows the correct prayer times for today and the upcoming week, giving you reliable reminders whether you are at home, work, or traveling.

Calendar entry details: Both individual and combined-prayer descriptions show only the OCR-fetched start time, with the date in day-month-year order: Bait us Subuh [Prayer name] Start Time: 06:20 on 02-11-2026. Individual calendar events last five minutes; combined-prayer calendar events have a native 10-minute range from the OCR time.

Method 1 · Important One-Time Step

Setting Prayer Alarms for Method 1

Because subscribed public feeds do not sound loud alarms on their own, follow this one-time setup to make your phone alert you before every prayer:

Google Calendar ➔ Menu → Settings ➔ Bait-us-Subuh Timings ➔ Add notification

Android · Google Calendar

  1. Open the Google Calendar app on your phone.
  2. Tap the Menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Settings (⚙️).
  4. Under the list of calendars, tap Bait-us-Subuh Timings.
  5. Tap Add a notification (under default notifications).
  6. Select your preferred reminder time (for example, 15 minutes before).
  7. Allow Google Calendar notifications in your Android phone settings.

iPhone / iPad · Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar in the Google account where you added the calendar.
  2. Tap Menu (☰) → Settings → Bait-us-Subuh Timings.
  3. Tap Add another notification and choose how many minutes before the event.
  4. In iPhone Settings, go to Notifications → Google Calendar and allow notifications and sounds.
🔔

What does this default rule do?

This is a calendar-level notification default for events on Bait-us-Subuh Timings in your account. Check that the reminder appears on future prayer events; it is not a phone alarm and does not override device notification permissions.

Reminder: Choose any lead time available in the calendar app. Method 1 does not set a fixed reminder time for you.

Phone Settings · Sound Customization

Setting Your Desired Ringtone (or Azaan MP3)

Important: The Prayer Times Automation app does not include a built-in sound player or audio files. All notification sounds are played directly by your phone's Google Calendar app.

In both methods, the phone's calendar app plays the alert. Android may let you choose a downloaded sound file. On iPhone, allow Google Calendar sounds in iOS notification settings; an arbitrary MP3 cannot be selected unless the app/iOS offers it as a notification sound.

Phone Settings (⚙️) ➔ Apps ➔ Google Calendar ➔ Notifications ➔ Sound ➔ Select Preferred Tone

Android Step-by-Step

  1. Open your Android phone's main Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps (or Apps & Notifications).
  3. Find and tap Calendar (Google Calendar).
  4. Tap Notifications ➔ tap Calendar notifications (or Event reminders).
  5. Ensure the alert style is set to Alerting (not Silent).
  6. Tap Sound.
  7. Select your preferred ringtone or your own downloaded audio file!
💡

How to use your own Azaan MP3 on your phone

If you have an Azaan MP3 audio file you wish to hear for prayers:

  1. Download or copy the MP3 file onto your phone.
  2. Using your phone's "Files" or "My Files" app, move it into the Notifications (or Ringtones) folder.
  3. It will now appear directly in your phone's sound selection list!

iPhone / iPad: Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Google Calendar, then allow notifications and sounds. Google Calendar on iOS does not provide an Android-style file browser for assigning an arbitrary downloaded MP3 to its notifications. Custom sounds are available only when iOS or the app exposes them as selectable notification sounds.

Devices · Compatibility

Device Setup: Android vs Apple iPhone

The app works on all modern smartphones. Here is what you need to know about your specific device:

Android Devices

Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, etc.

  • Installed by default: The Google Calendar app is pre-installed on virtually all certified Android phones.
  • If missing: If you ever uninstalled or disabled it, simply open the Google Play Store, search for Google Calendar, and tap install.
  • Audio customization: Android provides the most granular control over per-app notification sounds, making it easy to select an Azaan file.
Apple iPhone & iPad (iOS)

iOS Instructions

  • For Method 1 reminders: In the Google Calendar app, go to Menu → Settings → Bait-us-Subuh Timings → Add another notification and choose how early to be reminded.
  • Allow alerts: In iPhone Settings, open Notifications → Google Calendar and enable notifications and sounds.
  • Sound limitation: Google Calendar on iOS does not offer Android's browse-for-an-MP3 sound picker. An arbitrary Azan MP3 cannot be assigned unless the app/iOS exposes it as an available notification sound.

Summary: Use the Google Calendar app on Android or iPhone to manage the Bait-us-Subuh calendar. Menu labels may vary slightly by app and operating-system version.

Assistance · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from members.

🔊 I subscribed, but I am not hearing any sound?

For Method 1 (QR scan), ensure you completed the one-time step in Calendar Settings ➔ Bait-us-Subuh Timings ➔ Add notification (Slide 7). Also verify your phone is not on Do Not Disturb or Mute.

🔄 Will it keep updating automatically next week?

Yes! The mosque timetable engine continuously syncs updates. When next week's schedule is published, it refreshes your calendar without you having to scan again.

⏰ Can I change how early the reminder sounds?

Yes! In Google Calendar settings, you can change the notification to 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes before, or add multiple reminders.

❌ How do I disconnect or unsubscribe?

Method 1: In Google Calendar, select Bait-us-Subuh Timings in settings and tap Unsubscribe / Delete.
Method 2: Go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove access.
